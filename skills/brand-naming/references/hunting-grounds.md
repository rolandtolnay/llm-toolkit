# Hunting Grounds — Generation Territories and Seed Databases

This file is read by generation-team subagents. It is a map of dig sites, not a menu to pick one
from. Work through EVERY ground that plausibly connects to your brief; log everything you find,
including obvious trash. Quantity leads to quality — a 150-name list with 120 duds beats a
curated 30. You are mapping the whole seabed before anyone dives.

Rules while digging:
- **Speculate, don't evaluate.** Never discard a name for trademark, domain, or "too weird"
  reasons during generation. That filtering happens later, by someone else.
- **Approximate thinking.** Deliberately range from bizarre/absurd to safe. The best names live
  in the middle — approximate ideas, not fully baked, not conservative. If nothing on your list
  embarrasses you slightly, you stayed too safe.
- Every name gets a 3-8 word gloss (what it evokes / where it came from) and a ground tag.

## Construction patterns (apply within every ground)

| Pattern | Mechanism | Examples | Trademark strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real word, recontextualized | Ordinary word, surprising in category | Apple, Slack, Amazon, Windsurf, Feather | Strong (arbitrary) |
| Phrase / claim | Bold statement as name | Impossible, Slim Fast, Beyond | Strong, high energy |
| Compound | Two real words fused | BlackBerry, HubSpot, Facebook, SpaceX | Medium-strong |
| Blend / portmanteau | Head of A + tail of B | Pinterest, Microsoft, Febreze (fabric+breeze) | Strong |
| Affixed | Root + suffix (-ify, -io, -ly, -eo, -ora, -a) | Spotify, Shopify, Grammarly | Medium |
| Classical root build | Latin/Greek root shaped into a new word | Pentium, Sonos, Volvo ("I roll"), Audi ("listen!") | Strong (coined) |
| Pure coinage | Invented from phonesthemes/CVCV | Kodak, Xerox, Hulu | Strongest, needs meaning built |
| Borrowed foreign word | Real word from another language | Azure (via Persian/French), Lego ("leg godt"), Samsara | Strong |
| Mythic/scientific allusion | Named entity carrying story | Nike, Tesla, Atlas, Iridium | Strong |

Legal note for later stages (do NOT filter on this now): the spectrum descriptive → suggestive →
arbitrary → fanciful runs weakest-to-strongest in trademark protectability. "Fiber Plus" is
nearly unprotectable; "Feather" (arbitrary in fiber) and "Febreze" (coined) are highly
protectable. Comfortable descriptive names are weak legally AND invisible in market — same trap.

## Ground 3: Periodic table and science

Pentium came from this ground (pente = five, fifth-generation chip, + -ium element suffix).
Two moves: use element names/etymologies directly, or borrow the *morphology* (-ium, -on, -ide).

Elements with evocative etymologies: Neon ("new"), Xenon ("stranger"), Argon ("idle/lazy"),
Krypton ("hidden"), Helium (sun), Iridium (Iris/rainbow), Cobalt (goblin), Lithium (stone),

Science concepts that name well: Phase Change, Quark, Flux, Ion, Photon, Catalyst, Quantum,

## Ground 5: Expressions and idioms

Mine the phrases real people use around the ultimate benefit. For "feeling light": light as a
feather, walking on air, spring in your step, float, breeze through, weight off my shoulders,
featherweight. Names hide inside idioms (Feather came from here). Method: list 10-15 idioms and
colloquial expressions around the benefit, extract every noun/verb, and treat each as a
candidate or blend material. The user's own words in `raw_qa` are prime material — founders
often say the name in the interview without noticing.

## Ground 6: Cross-domain expert vocabularies

The "spend 15 minutes on what makes an airplane lift" move. Pick 2-3 physical or expert domains
where the ultimate benefit is *literally engineered*, then mine their jargon. For lightness →
aviation/aerodynamics: Lift, Camber, Glide, Soar, Aileron, Slipstream, Tailwind, Mach, Vector,
Aero, Wing, Feather (a propeller term too), Loft, Hover, Buoyant. For speed → racing, falconry,
sprinting. For trust → locksmithing, mountaineering (anchor, belay), banking vaults. For
connection → weaving, railways, telegraphy. Domain jargon is surprisingly familiar: real words,
never seen in your category.

## Ground 7: Synchronicity dig

The random-magazine move: creativity is connecting seemingly irrelevant things. Pick 2 domains
UNRELATED to both product and benefit and skim their vocabularies for anything that sparks
against the brief. Domain seeds: cooking, sailing, jazz, architecture, chess, tailoring,
gardening, mountaineering, typography, watchmaking, falconry, surfing, ceramics, cartography,
railways, beekeeping, calligraphy, orchestral music, cinema, carpentry, astronomy, perfumery,
bookbinding, glassblowing. (Windsurf — a code editor named from board sports — is this ground
working.) Force yourself to log 10+ candidates per domain even when the connection feels absurd;
absurd is inside the approximate-thinking range.

## Ground 8: Borrowed foreign words

Short, real words from other languages carrying the concept beautifully: Italian/Spanish
(vela=sail, ala=wing, sol, luna, onda=wave, cielo=sky, brio, lieve=light), Japanese
(sora=sky, kumo=cloud, hana=flower, yume=dream, kaze=wind, hikari=light), Nordic
(fjell=mountain, lys=light, luft=air, saga), Hawaiian/Polynesian (moana=ocean, mana, lani=sky).
Generate per-concept from your own knowledge — you speak these languages; that is an advantage
Lexicon's linguists charge for. Flag every borrowed word for meaning verification in screening.

## Ground 9: Pure coinage

Build from sound up (see sound-symbolism.md for the physics):
- **CVCV(C) builds**: pick target sounds for the strategy, assemble pronounceable skeletons
  (Sonos, Hulu, Vevo, Kodak pattern). Generate 20+ mechanically, keep what sings.
- **Phonestheme-guided**: English sound clusters carry meaning — gl- (light: gleam, glow),
  fl- (flight: float, flow), sw- (smooth motion: swift, sweep, swoosh), cr- (structure: crisp),
  sn- (nose/sneaky — avoid), -ash (violent: crash, smash). Start from the phonestheme matching
  the strategy and build outward.
- **Blends**: head of word A + tail of word B from ANY other ground (fabric + breeze = Febreze).
  Cross-pollinate your own list: take your best 10 real-word finds and blend pairs.

## Output format (per team)

One name per line, grouped by ground:

```
## Ground 1: Latin/Greek roots
- Levia — from levis (light); soft, feminine, pharma-adjacent
- Alev — levis reversed-ish; short, CVCV-adjacent, unexpected
...
```

End the file with a `## Sparks` section: 3-5 observations about which grounds felt rich and any
direction the convergence stage should know about (e.g., "the aviation ground kept producing
names that also imply speed — may be a two-benefit territory").
